Privacy policy

This policy covers the open-source desktop app and eido.so. Earlier desktop builds had different privacy settings. Cloudflare hosts the website and downloads, including update files for earlier versions.

Short version

Eido keeps your Profile on your device. Editing, exports, and local CLI and MCP access work offline. There is no Eido account. Eido collects no product analytics. An AI run can send prompts, connected card content, and attachments to the provider you configure. Online AI and search use your own API keys or your installed AI tool's provider access.

1. Who this policy covers

Eido is the Australian operator of the Eido desktop application, eido.so, and updates.eido.so. In this policy, “Eido”, “we”, and “us” mean that operator.

We use the practical standards in the Australian Privacy Principles as our baseline for everyone. Where the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) or another privacy law applies, we also follow the obligations that law places on us. This policy is notice. Agreeing to it is not consent to unrelated or new uses of information.

This policy covers information handled by Eido. Services you connect using your own account or API key also have their own terms and privacy practices.

2. What stays on your device

The installed Eido Profile is a local SQLite database. It can hold your Ideas, boards, cards, connections, attachments, prompts, generated results, conversations, Mods, history, settings, and activity or provenance records. Eido does not operate a hosted Profile database, cloud sync service, or account service.

Your OpenRouter and Linkup API keys are stored outside the Profile and are excluded from Portable Exports. They are kept in local files protected by the operating system's encrypted storage and file permissions. A locked protected store leaves AI credentials unavailable while your local board keeps working. Device security and access to your operating-system account remain part of the protection boundary.

You can create a Portable Export of the logical Profile and its referenced assets. It does not include API keys, SQLite working files, or every device-only setting. Local agents and command-line tools you invoke can make bounded, allowlisted reads or changes to the installed Profile; their activity can be recorded in it.

3. When information leaves your device

Eido sends run material to AI and web providers when you start a run. Displaying a link preview can also make a request to the site the link points to.

You run AI
Eido can send the compiled prompt, relevant connected card content, selected settings, and included images or files.
OpenRouter routes the model you choose to a downstream provider selected under OpenRouter's routing and your account settings, or a locally invoked AI tool handles the request under its own provider account. You start the run.
You read, search, or research the web
Eido can send the public page address, search question, page request, or research brief needed for the action.
Linkup, OpenRouter, or a routed provider performs the action, depending on the feature and credentials you use.
Eido displays a link preview
Eido can request the public page and its preview images directly to display the link's title, description, thumbnail, and favicon. An existing preview can refresh when you open a board.
The page and image hosts receive the requested addresses and ordinary network information.
You visit the website or download a release
Cloudflare receives ordinary connection information such as IP address, request time, requested page or file, and network or browser information.
Cloudflare serves and protects the website, installers, and signed update files for older app versions.

Eido does not proxy AI or web requests through an Eido server. The request goes from your device to the service performing the action. Context can include more than the text visible in one card, because Eido may compile connected material and attachments for the run.

Eido does not ask you to place sensitive personal information in a board or prompt. Keep sensitive information local unless you understand the recipient's terms and have authority to disclose it, especially where it concerns another person.

4. Providers and their jobs

We name material providers rather than hide them behind “trusted partners”. Their policies can change, and the selected AI route can use additional subprocessors. Check the provider controls connected to your own account before sending sensitive material.

OpenRouter and routed model providers
Perform online AI runs you start using your OpenRouter API key. OpenRouter receives the compiled run material and routes it to the selected model provider. Attachments may use separate tools: Eido's current PDF path asks OpenRouter to use Cloudflare AI for parsing. Retention and model-training rules depend on your OpenRouter settings, the selected endpoint, and enabled tools. Eido does not currently force Zero Data Retention on the request. Read OpenRouter's privacy policy, provider information, and ZDR controls.
Linkup
Performs web search, page fetch, or research when you add a Linkup key and use those tools. It can receive the search question, public page address, filters, and research brief. A submitted research job cannot currently be cancelled at Linkup; Eido's Cancel action stops waiting and polling. Read Linkup's privacy policy.
Cloudflare
Serves and protects eido.so, installers, and signed update files for older app versions. It processes ordinary network and security metadata. The site is static and Eido adds no advertising or website-analytics scripts. Cloudflare also participates in PDF parsing when that OpenRouter feature is used. Read Cloudflare's privacy policy.
AI tools installed by you
Eido can invoke supported tools such as Claude Code, Codex, or Antigravity on your device. “Locally invoked” does not necessarily mean local inference: the tool may send prompts and attachments to its provider using your existing account. That provider's terms and privacy practices apply.

5. What we handle and why

Local Profile and settings

Eido's software handles Profile content and device settings to save, display, edit, export, recover, and run the features you request. The operator does not receive a hosted copy through ordinary local use.

AI and web requests

Eido sends the request material to provide the action you started. Where EEA or UK law applies, the usual basis is taking steps at your request or performing our agreement with you. Separate consent may be needed for sensitive information or another purpose.

Website and downloads

Routine request data is processed to deliver pages and files, maintain availability, and protect the service. If EEA or UK law applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in secure, reliable delivery where those interests are not overridden, and on legal obligations where required.

6. Overseas processing

Eido is operated from Australia. Cloudflare uses a global network. OpenRouter and some model providers process in the United States or other countries shown in their current provider information. Linkup states that its processing occurs in the European Union. Local AI tools can use the locations disclosed by their provider.

The countries involved can change with the model, endpoint, tool, subprocessor, and your account settings. Where cross-border privacy rules apply to Eido, we take reasonable steps and will put an applicable lawful transfer mechanism in place before Eido initiates a transfer that requires one. A privacy policy is not itself a transfer safeguard.

7. Retention and deletion

Local Profile data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, replace the Profile, remove application data, or the operating system removes it. Deleting an Idea removes it from the active logical Profile, but limited recovery generations, tombstones, blob material, quarantined files, or operating-system backups may remain for a time. Eido does not promise immediate secure erasure of every physical copy.

API keys remain in their separate local files until you clear them or remove the relevant application data. Clearing a key does not promise forensic secure erasure from storage media or backups.

Provider retention follows the provider, endpoint, account settings, and contracts described above. We retain legal records only while reasonably needed for safety, disputes, or legal obligations.

8. Security

Eido uses measures intended to protect the product boundary, including an isolated desktop renderer, a restrictive content security policy, permission-protected credential files, defensive SQLite settings, and bounded local agent interfaces. No software or storage method is perfectly secure. Your device security, operating-system account, connected providers, and backups remain part of the protection boundary.

If a data breach affects personal information Eido controls, we will assess it and notify affected people and regulators where required by law, and otherwise where notice would help people protect themselves.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Keep ordinary board work local by not starting online AI or web actions.
  • Choose which supported AI route or tool handles a run.
  • Clear stored API keys.
  • Create a Portable Export of your Profile.
  • Edit or delete Ideas in the active Profile, subject to the local recovery limits above.
  • Ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict, or explain information Eido controls, where reasonably possible and legally required.
  • Complain about our handling of personal information.

Eido does not require an account. The operator does not hold your local Profile, so we usually cannot access, correct, or delete that local content for you. A request may require enough information to verify your identity and find the relevant record without exposing more data than necessary. Legal rights are subject to exceptions; if we refuse a request, we will explain why where the law requires it.

10. Children

Eido is for people aged 18 or older, or the age of legal majority where they live. It is not directed to children. Do not use Eido to send a child's personal information to an online provider unless you are legally authorised and the provider permits it. If we become aware that a child has provided information to Eido's operator, we will investigate.

11. AI and automated decisions

Eido's current AI features help generate, organise, and explore material. Eido does not use personal information to make eligibility, employment, credit, health, insurance, or similarly significant automated decisions about people. AI output can still affect a person if a user chooses to rely on it. Review important output and keep a person responsible for consequential decisions.

12. Regional information

Australia

We aim to apply the practical protections described in this policy to everyone. If the Privacy Act applies to our handling, you may have rights under the Australian Privacy Principles. You can complain to us first and, where the Act applies, to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom

If EU GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you may have rights to information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, consent withdrawal, and safeguards for certain significant automated decisions. You may complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work. These rights are not absolute. Australia is not treated as adequate by the EU or UK, so applicable transfers need a separate lawful mechanism. If Eido begins targeting these markets in a way that requires an EU or UK representative, we will publish that representative's details before doing so.

California and other United States states

If a state privacy law applies, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or receive certain information, and to opt out of certain sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling practices. Eido does not use personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising and does not receive payment for personal information. Whether a vendor relationship has a statutory “sale” or “sharing” label depends on the law, contract, and actual use. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable right.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date and keep prior material versions available when practical. If a change materially expands what Eido collects, why it is used, or who receives it, we will give a clear notice before the new practice begins where practical and obtain consent where law requires it. We will not treat this policy as blanket permission for an unrelated use.

## Inputs

- Goal and lane contract: <pointer>
- Exact stable delta or artifact: <pointer and identity>
- Claims made: <behavior, compatibility, evidence>

## Standards review

Check architecture, repository conventions, safety, maintainability, affected
consumers, and whether validation cost is proportional to risk.

## Spec review

Check the promised outcome, preserved product truths, exclusions, undeclared
behavior, and whether the evidence directly observes each material claim.

```
VERDICT: PASS | BLOCK
BLOCKING FINDINGS: <file/artifact location, consequence, proof needed>
```

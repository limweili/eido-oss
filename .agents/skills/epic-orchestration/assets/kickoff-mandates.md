# Kickoff alignment

The bare goal should be enough to begin. Resolve only missing decisions that
materially change the work:

- Why does the outcome matter, and what harm does failure create?
- Which product and safety truths are inviolable?
- What observable state means the epic is done?
- What authority exists for integration, publication, and cleanup?
- Which owner decisions justify interruption?
- What evidence is authoritative under the available conditions?

Changing tools, execution topology, and ordinary validation mechanics do not
require rewriting the goal. Capture them in the live plan or repository
policy only while they remain useful.

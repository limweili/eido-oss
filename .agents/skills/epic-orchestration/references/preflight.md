# Orientation — establish reality before slicing

The purpose of orientation is to prevent the epic from optimizing an obsolete
plan or asking unavailable evidence to prove the work.

## Re-establish the destination

- Read the canonical goal or specification and preserve its why, product
  truths, safety boundaries, exclusions, and definition of done.
- Identify which instructions are durable outcomes and which are tactics that
  may change as execution teaches you more.
- Resolve contradictory sources in favor of explicit owner intent and current
  product reality.

## Re-establish live state

- Derive issue, dependency, change, integration, and delivery state from the
  current systems that own it.
- Inspect concurrent work and shared surfaces before claiming parallelism.
- Treat saved plans and ledgers as recovery aids; refresh every mutable claim
  that affects a decision.

## Establish capability and authority

- Determine what execution, review, validation, and landing capabilities are
  actually available for this run.
- Classify environment-sensitive evidence before depending on it. An
  incapable or saturated host must not become a recurring product gate.
- Confirm who may mutate shared state and which decisions require the owner.

## Design the evidence economy

For each material uncertainty, name:

- the claim that must be known;
- the cheapest direct evidence that can discriminate it;
- existing evidence that can be reused;
- what kind of change would invalidate that proof;
- the stop condition after the decision is resolved.

Orientation is complete when the destination, live dependency map, authority,
available capabilities, and evidence plan are clear enough to choose the first
valuable frontier without guessing.

# Eido

## Where ideas find their form.

An idea is not always something you have. Sometimes it is something you are still trying to see.

Eido is a thinking space for thoughts that are too alive to sit neatly in notes. Capture fragments, connect what matters, shape an idea as it changes, and use AI to extend your thinking without taking it away from you.

The word comes from *eidos*: form, shape, essence — from the same ancient root as *idea*.

Before an idea becomes clear, it often begins as a glimpse. Eido is where that glimpse takes shape..

What am I'm solving? 
- my thoughts lately feels like they are formed and shaped. 
- I don't know what the final goal is, but thinking about it, and putting it down somewhere is the start of shaping it. 
- intense thought is extremely tiring to keep all in my head, using ai directly make it even more tiring as it creates more things I need to review and critique. 
- also, ai just agrees with my ideas and latches on the current session prompt. It's so much work to give it all context. Even if I had a repo where the agent can read through first doesn't solve it as each time we work on something it'll miss connections if not precisely documented. My brain doesn't document, it shapes thought in real time. By the time wait for a response to generate something I've shaped my thought 100 times. 
- live visual editor tools allow real time shaping. The feedback loop is real time. I need to "see" my thought to shape it. 
- AI can find the 10 variations to an idea but it can't create the idea I have in mind. It can't possibly do that for 2 reasons. First, I've got way more influencing instruction that I can't prompt ai. These influences can be strong or it can be weak. It can even be negative. To express a thought, I might have 10-20 influences each with its own other influences. Then to form a concrete idea, it may need many thoughts or even a change of mind. Second, I don't know what the idea is, it is unknowable only iteratbable. The idea is never final. Only the final output is final. The adherence to the original goal is a stop condition that only a user can know once it is reached. Reaching it is the whole point.  
- I'm solving for a thinking tool, a thinking partner. I want the best of both me and AI. Me: creative and inspired. AI: generative and infinte. 
- I want to feel like I can get ai to think on my wavelength, to read my mind where context is holistic, and not a single prompt. 
- I need to be able to accelerate thought, not replace it. Using ai tools these days feel like I'm replacing thoughts. Of course it produces slop if it just did it for me. I don't want it to do it for me, I want ai to accelerate me. 
- Most importantly, it should feel effortless. Part of the effortless trap is to get ai to generate a bunch of shit, it's mostly shit I'll now have to read and review. Taking time away from thinking and spending time weeding out slop instead. Effortless means precise thought generation, it assists connections and generation but never replaces the intent.

Benefits of this vs writing regular notes, why use this?
- inspiration can come from anywhere, and any time, this is a form of capture and connection. 
- ideas get the space to be iterated on, rather than filed away. 
- ideas have a sense of timeline transformation, super important as the evolution and passage of iteration in itself is context. 
- it should cut mental load of keeping organised note. Unorganised notes help drive inspiration. Organisation stifles creativity. 
- it needs to be formed around the way the users brain work. Neat or messy, logical or subjective, whatever the spectrum, this needs to support. 
- the user should feel like they have the tools to expand ideas and ideate at the speed of thought.

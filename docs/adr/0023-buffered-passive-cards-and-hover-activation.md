# ADR 0023: Buffered Passive Cards And Hover Activation

Status: accepted; buffered passive residency, safety, and Live activation production since 2026-07-19

Buffered rich-DOM residency with hysteresis, direction-aware look-ahead, the real 540-Card ceiling, and the existing Board-wide lightweight safety representation are the production editable-Board path. Independently pinned Live Cards, exactly-once first-intent activation, and complete long-Card Result/editor continuity are now production too; the separate bounded gesture/proximity overlay remains downstream of the cold scene. Board search and whole-Board copy derive from the saved graph rather than resident DOM, so semantic completeness does not require every rich Card to mount.

Reconciliation note (2026-07-23): issue #753 adds a 300,000-unit cumulative per-viewport weight ceiling to cold `P`. Exact-visible Cards remain the first deterministic/hysteretic admission tier, but `V` is no longer an unconditional subset: `V − P` stays in painted `S` at rest. Independently owned `L` is exempt and joins visual occlusion/suppression downstream without entering cold residency history. Graph-derived search/copy/focus remain complete across this partition.

Field reconciliation note (2026-07-23): founder testing exposed head-of-line starvation when a single oversize candidate ended the strict prefix while most of the budget remained unused. Cold `P` now scans the same deterministic tiers and prior-resident-first order for an ordered subsequence within 600,000 units. A weight-rejected Card remains in `S` and later ordered candidates remain eligible; Card-count exhaustion may still end admission. `L`, activation, and painter authority are unchanged.

Field reconciliation note (2026-07-23): a founder-scale 609,000-unit Note showed that painter-only rest presentation is not acceptable for an exact-visible oversize Card even when hover activation works. A separate viewport-local pool `O` therefore admits at most 10 exact-visible Cards whose individual weight exceeds 600,000. `O` uses the existing stable prior-visible-first order, consumes the shared 540-Card ceiling, never spends the ordinary 600,000-unit pool, and never admits look-ahead, preload, or offscreen retention. Rich DOM remains one owner across `P ∪ O ∪ L`; the 10-Card worst case is founder-manual evidence, not permission to raise the cap without a real 10-giant viewport result.

Field reconciliation note (2026-08-21): after the full native-DOM motion path was made content-cold and founder testing found 100,000 units already exceptional in real Boards, `O` begins strictly above 100,000 individual text-weight units and admits at most 10 exact-visible Cards. A follow-up 30-Card trial was withdrawn after founder testing found 30 simultaneous ~281,000-unit Cards unusable. The ordinary `P` budget remains 600,000 cumulative units, `P ∪ O` still shares the terminal 540-Card ceiling, and `O` remains unavailable to look-ahead, preload, and offscreen retention.

Motion reconciliation note (2026-08-20): founder testing rejected hiding long-Card text during pan, zoom, or drag. Every resident rich Card kind now keeps its native DOM visible while semantic Card subscriptions exclude position and stacking fields. React Flow moves only the shallow node plane; zoom-dependent chrome values subscribe at their small leaves, and Reading crossfade uses standard non-inherited opacity on the exact visual-node wrapper. No changing Board custom property inherits through rich Card prose. This preserves trusted interaction, focus, selection, internal scrolling, browser Find, and accessibility while avoiding descendant rerender and inherited-style work. A DOM-to-canvas motion overlay was measured and removed because its cover transitions introduced gesture-boundary stalls; the terminal rejection of cached passive Cards therefore remains intact. A separate 1,024-entry true-LRU keyed by Idea and Card restores reading position across ordinary rich-DOM eviction/remount without entering the saved graph or Profile; stale entries may be evicted. Packaged post-drop capture remains a separate, after-paint durability seam.

Soft-containment reconciliation note (2026-08-21): #1157 selected DELETE after the durable #1154 source study and #1156 Card-root negative control found no exact-visible material win and could not measure the mandatory headed/native Find gate. #1168 removed the four disposable experiment artifacts under `experiments/issue-1156-soft-containment`; no `content-visibility` production path, flag, or second renderer is authorized. The legacy #405 cached-passive source lineage, build flag, workflow, and publication harness are now removed. The complete native Card DOM and additive `P ∪ O ∪ L` ownership described here remain the sole Reading interaction authority.

Closeout reconciliation note (2026-08-21): #1165 rejected the proposed individually-oversized own-drag body blank/fade and retained the complete native body for every resident Card kind. The paired motion authority now runs both raw `AB` and `BA` control/stress orders and gates each stress phase and aggregate against the same-run short-control envelope (`max(control max + 2 cadence + 2, control max × 1.5)`), with control-relative p95, missed-frame, long-task, and completion bounds; the former absolute host maximum is not a production cutoff. Selection authorizes editor entry, but a focused editor survives dragging another Card and ends only on real blur, a tool/scope change, or a stationary click on another Card. Native browser toolbar Find is inherited from the unchanged complete visible DOM; `window.find` smoke is not toolbar automation. Packaged drag persistence remains after the released geometry paints, with coalesced capture and checkpoint flush preserving the latest exact state.

Geometry reconciliation note (2026-08-21): the first settled complete native render of a Card without an authored size now establishes its durable `Card.size`. Card-local border-box observation waits for fonts and a short settle window, then batches adoption into one persisted graph change without undo, Intent history, or authorship movement; an existing explicit size always wins. The same saved footprint drives native `P ∪ O ∪ L` and painted `S`, so demotion, activation, and reload cannot resize the Card. This is durable geometry, not another renderer or a residency rule.

Interaction reconciliation note (2026-08-21): hover is presentation/geometry-only for every Card and never creates Live ownership. Exact-visible `O` and painter-owned `S` presses defer Live ownership until React Flow resolves the shared 5px click/drag threshold. Pointer travel remains geometry-only and cannot select the Card, mount an editor, or show loading. A stationary click changes only React Flow's shallow wrapper selection, whose paint-only green frame renders without changing the rich Card's component or data identity. After one browser paint opportunity, semantic interaction selection and accepted Live/editor work follow while stale rapid choices cancel. The outgoing Card retains its editor authority through that visual deselection paint, then releases it. A newly mounted `S` Card receives the already-resolved point intent. The existing Card-colour dot's design-system pulse begins with the loading marker and remains perceptible for at least 500 ms from transition start unless reduced motion is enabled; genuine editor readiness is never delayed. Ordinary `P`, already-Live Cards, and drag gestures never pulse.

## Context

ADR 0019 established Eido's successful Reading → Overview → Map fidelity, and ADR 0020 established its painter and interaction invariants. Source studies of Excalidraw and tldraw show two different ways to keep navigation responsive: Excalidraw culls and reuses cached element rasters, while tldraw retains every shape DOM subtree and synchronously hides or reveals it. Eido's Cards are substantially heavier than ordinary whiteboard shapes, so retaining every rich Card as live DOM does not scale, but exact-edge unloading can expose blank or disjointed regions during a fast pan.

The founder accepts a short load after a rare far jump, but ordinary and exceptionally fast navigation must still feel like the whole Board is present. The founder also rejects both a two-click passive-Card experience and hover-triggered heavy work: hover may enrich presentation and geometry, while one resolved stationary click must select immediately and deliver the intended Live interaction after the selection paint.

## Decision

Eido preserves the Detail Band fidelity contract while separating **Board existence**, **motion representation**, **passive Reading residency**, **live interaction authority**, and **active editing**. Passive and Live describe lifecycle and authority; they do not select cached pixels versus DOM. The production passive renderer is ordinary buffered rich DOM:

1. The whole Board retains lightweight Card/Connection graph records, spatial bounds, and its Overview/Map-capable canvas representation. During Reading navigation this representation remains available as a never-blank motion safety net beneath detailed Cards.
2. Ordinary rich DOM occupies a viewport-derived resident window with screen-space preload margin, hysteresis, bounded direction-aware look-ahead, the 540-Card ceiling, and a 600,000-unit cumulative text-weight ceiling. Exact-visible Cards are considered first; a weight-rejected Card remains represented by `S` rather than being discarded or changing the band, and does not starve later ordered candidates. Separately, `O` admits at most 10 exact-visible Cards whose individual weight exceeds 100,000 units, without look-ahead, preload, or retention.
   A text-bearing Reading `S` Card paints the existing cached plain-text summary as a bounded body excerpt (90 source characters, at most six lines per Card, and the existing viewport-area text-line cap across the frame). This prevents an otherwise recognisable Card frame from appearing empty without changing residency: the excerpt is canvas-only visual context, never Markdown, DOM, link, selection, Find, clipboard, or accessibility authority, and it disappears when `P`, `O`, or `L` mounts the native Card.
3. Hover is presentation/geometry-only and never begins **Card activation**. Mounted `P` retains native focus and first-intent behavior without adding a hover owner. Programmatic focus/selection may activate painter-owned `S` directly for keyboard and accessibility entry. A direct `S` body press supplies the normal single-click Card-selection path; if it resolves as a stationary click, visual selection paints before the Live/editor handoff and the original point reaches the mounted native surface without duplicate synthetic replay. Pointer travel remains geometry-only. Editing, internal scrolling, active media, focus, and accepted interaction owners pin a Card independently.
4. A Live Card keeps normal Reading behavior, including full internal navigation, links, selection, editing, keyboard operation, and scrolling for long Notes, Conversations, and Results. Long Results retain complete Markdown before activation. Selection authorizes a Markdown editor to open only when the pointer resolves on the click side of the shared click/drag threshold, then enters at the recorded release without a competing root-end selection or relative-scroll loss. Pointer travel of `O`/`S` remains geometry-only: it neither selects nor acquires Live/editor ownership. Once focused, the editor survives dragging another Card and ends only on real blur, a tool/scope change, or a stationary click on another Card. Demotion happens only after every owner ends and ordinary residency or a replacement is ready, keeps the saved native footprint exactly, and cannot delay visual deselection.
5. An arbitrary jump into an unprepared distant region may briefly show the lightweight Board representation while detailed Cards load. This is an accepted rare-case fidelity step-down; a blank Board is not. Ordinary and exceptionally fast navigation must never expose blank Board regions.
6. Rich-DOM population and decoded/derived media have explicit, independently measured budgets and eviction rules. Repeated traversal must not grow memory without bound.

### Runtime sets and precedence

The selected architecture uses seven distinct authorities:

- **Viewport-visible (`V`)**: Cards whose Reading representation intersects the current viewport under the existing scene and LOD policy.
- **Preload-resident (`P`)**: the deterministic, hysteretic ordered subsequence admitted from exact-visible `V`, bounded directional look-ahead, preload, and recently departed Cards, in that priority order, within both cold ceilings. `P` is the ordinary rich-DOM residency set; candidates that cross the remaining weight stay in `S`, while reaching the Card-count ceiling ends admission.
- **Oversize-visible (`O`)**: at most 10 exact-visible Cards whose individual weight exceeds 100,000 units, admitted prior-visible-first. `O` has no look-ahead, preload, or offscreen retention and is separately counted/weighed; further exact-visible oversized Cards remain in painter-owned `S`.
- **Live owners (`L`)**: Reading Cards pinned by accepted activation, focus, interaction-ready selection, editing, internal scrolling, active media, keyboard focus, or an eligible resident gesture. Hover is excluded for every Card. `O`/`S` pointer travel is also excluded until a stationary click resolves. Owners are additive and pin independently of `P`.
- **Hot-overlay proxies (`G`)**: at most 0–2 lightweight or invisible hover/proximity or pointer-manipulation producers. `G` is neither rich Card DOM nor Live authority. Hover alone never starts or retains `L`; an independently eligible resident gesture may retain its existing Live owner, while `O`/`S` manipulation remains geometry-only.
- **Active editors (`E`)**: Cards owning an editor, caret, selection, or IME/composition session. By definition, `E ⊆ L`.
- **Safety representations (`S`)**: lightweight Board-wide painted Cards that prevent blank regions and own canvas body selection/activation entry while claiming neither rich descendant semantics nor Live interaction authority.

Rich-DOM residency is **`P ∪ O ∪ L`**, not `P ∪ O ∪ L ∪ G ∪ E`. The implementation unions those ids before mounting, so overlapping cold/Live ownership never creates a second DOM Card. `E` is already inside `L`; `G` remains a separate bounded overlay; and `S` remains independently available across the Board. Live pins win over residency eviction.

Rich-Card motion is not an eighth authority or a residency set. Position and stacking fields stay outside Card semantic subscriptions, and camera/Card movement remains a shallow React Flow transform. Zoom-scale values are local to the connector, resize, and connection chrome leaves that consume them; Reading opacity is a standard non-inherited property on the visual-node wrapper. The semantic subtree therefore does not rerender or inherit a changing custom property on each gesture frame. While resident, native DOM stays visible and remains the only hit, focus, selection, scroll, media, browser Find, and accessibility authority. Within-Card virtualization, motion-only visibility suppression, and cached DOM-to-canvas motion pixels are not used.

Scroll retention is independent of motion rendering and residency authority. Its 1,024-entry true-LRU is keyed by `(Idea id, Card id)`, restores exact offsets across same-surface remounts and relative position across passive/editor shape changes, and may evict stale entries. It is deliberately absent from graph/Profile data. Gesture frames likewise remain renderer-local; packaged gesture-summary capture is coalesced until after the released geometry paints, while checkpoint flush preserves the latest exact durable state.

### Invalidation, release, and first intent

Production enables the hot owner state machine only for editable Boards while Reading opacity is positive. Hover remains CSS/painter presentation for every Card and never enters `L` or wakes Board hover state. Existing ordinary resident targets retain native focus and pointer intent. `O` and painter-owned `S` latch presses as click-resolved: React Flow may move their shallow node/proxy, but pointer travel is filtered out of `L`, and only a stationary click publishes visual selection. A nested animation-frame barrier gives that selection one paint opportunity before interaction selection, owner release/acquisition, and accepted Live/editor work swap; stale pending choices cancel. Programmatic focus/selection remains a direct accessibility path. The mount plan composes accepted `L` after the cold scene, so an owner outside `P` stays mounted without entering residency history. It enters the visual-DOM occlusion set only once native ownership exists; until then the painter remains authoritative, preventing a blank handoff. At zero Reading opacity the owner state machine is disabled and the same bounded `G` may continue only as a lightweight proxy.

Mounted rich Cards are named Card groups whose accessible names expose kind, label, and selection state. `G` is aria-hidden and unfocusable. Programmatic Card focus selects and centres the graph target, waits for the exact rich wrapper to become keyboard-reachable, and then focuses it so focus capture pins `L`; the one-request observer cancels if another connected control receives focus first. If a focused Card is removed without a live replacement focus, the named Board region is the recovery target. This mounted-Card seam does not imply that every Card is resident or simultaneously present in the accessibility tree: the bounded graph-derived Board search surface is the single off-residency keyboard and assistive-technology entry, and stable result activation reuses the same focus request.

- `V` follows authoritative scene/LOD membership and changes with camera, viewport, Card geometry, band, occupancy, deadband, and crossfade state. Leaving `V` does not immediately evict rich DOM.
- `P` changes with the camera, spatial-index result, buffer and direction policy, geometry, per-Card weight projection, LOD membership, hysteresis, and settlement. Previous residents lead within each admission tier so small boundary motion is stable; release still requires absence from `L`.
- `L` changes only with explicit owner lifecycles. Content, theme, or geometry updates may rerender a Live Card but do not clear ownership. Release waits for every owner to end; ending `E` removes only the editor owner, not unrelated owners.
- `G` changes with hover/proximity identity and pointer start, move, completion, cancellation, endpoint, geometry, or transform. These hot geometry/presentation changes do not themselves create `L`. Any matching Live owner comes from an independent accepted lifecycle and releases on that lifecycle's terms.
- `S` changes with saved Card existence, appearance, geometry, or authoritative scene state and is replaced atomically. It remains available outside `P ∪ O ∪ L` and never becomes graph truth.
- Hit authority follows the representation owner. DOM handles `P ∪ O ∪ L`; canvas body candidates handle `S`; complementary underlays and invisible proxies cannot retain a second unintended target.

### Cost ownership and budgets

Board-global work is limited to saved graph records, spatial bounds/indexes, viewport-independent metrics, the lightweight `S` scene, and aggregate budget accounting. These projections remain rebuildable from the one saved graph and must not be recomputed by pointer, typing, streaming, or animation-frame activity. The visible-Card occupancy threshold remains authoritative for whole-viewport band demotion. Board-wide `boardDetailTextWeight` remains observable; its immutable per-Card map is now the eligibility input for cold `P`, so text weight can leave individual Cards painted but can never demote the whole viewport.

Resident/object-local work includes rich DOM, Markdown construction, style/layout/paint for `P ∪ O ∪ L`, editor work for `E`, and image decode/rendition work for actual visible, preloading, or Live visual consumers. Production ordinary `P` is bounded by the existing 540-Card Reading ceiling and the 600,000-unit text-weight ceiling. It considers `V` before preload-only Cards, skips a candidate that would cross the remaining weight, and continues in deterministic order. `O` adds at most 10 exact-visible Cards above the 100,000-unit individual threshold, counts against 540, and reports its combined actual weight separately; further oversized Cards remain in `S`, and `L` remains exempt. The #403 prototype reduced measured peak resident Cards from 131 to 80 during ordinary pan and 72 during exceptionally fast pan, but its 131-Card fixture did not exercise either final ceiling. Those observations remain provisional evidence for the residency seam, not a universal DOM or device promise.

Versions, provenance, settings, and other cold data remain a secondary, neutral lane for #408. Their loading policy cannot substitute for or change `V`, `P`, `L`, `G`, `E`, `S`, or image authority.

### Compatibility obligations

Native browser toolbar Find observes mounted DOM only and therefore does not discover an unmounted Card. For a mounted Card it is inherited from the unchanged complete visible native DOM; Eido does not claim independent toolbar-UI automation, and `window.find` smoke is not a substitute for that browser surface. That limitation is recorded, not counted as a performance benefit. Eido's Cmd/Ctrl+F Board search is the complete product route: its rebuildable semantic projection reads immutable saved Card order, Card text and Mod fields, asset names, every saved result version/transcript/follow-up, user-visible run provenance, and connection semantics. The visible and accessibility result list is paged in a bounded window; stable ordering and Previous/Next traversal keep every matching Card reachable without rebuilding whole-Board DOM.

Cmd/Ctrl+A creates a logical whole-Board copy scope and deliberately does not populate React Flow selection or `L`. Cmd/Ctrl+C serializes complete selected graph semantics—including offscreen Cards, saved result history, user-visible run provenance, and internal selected connections—through the shared clipboard boundary while retaining the existing in-app Card paste payload. Binary asset data URLs, durable ids, timestamps, and other implementation bookkeeping are graph authority but not user content and are intentionally excluded from the text serialization. A non-collapsed visible text selection continues through the native copy path unchanged.

The bounded search result surface plus mounted focus seam are the production keyboard and accessibility route across residency: every Card remains reachable through stable graph identity and bounded paging; activation focuses the exact named rich Card only after it mounts; focus pins `L`; `G` is excluded; and orphan recovery never steals a valid replacement focus. Unmounted Cards are deliberately not tab stops and are not all materialized in the accessibility tree at once. Link activation and long-Card interaction continuity remain mandatory production behavior; missing evidence is `not measured`, never an accepted sacrifice.

## Considered Options

- Chosen: #403's rich-DOM residency seam expanded from an exact viewport cut to buffered preload, bounded look-ahead, hysteresis, owner pins, and the Board-wide safety representation.
- Not chosen: tldraw-style retention of every rich Card DOM subtree; it preserves warm interaction at whole-Board memory and construction cost.
- Not chosen: an exact viewport cut by itself; direction reversal and rare far-jump evidence made it too brittle without buffering, hysteresis, and `S`.
- Terminally rejected: [#404 within-Card content visibility](../../research/03-performance-architecture/17-within-card-virtualization-candidate.md). Scroll drift, live-to-passive re-entry drift, and first-intent retargeting are correctness failures; no timing result overrides them.
- Terminally rejected: [#405 cached passive Cards](../../research/03-performance-architecture/18-cached-passive-rich-card-candidate.md). Replacing passive rich DOM with pixels blocks trusted native activation, exact selection/copy, normal keyboard traversal, descendant-link semantics, complete accessibility structure, and retained browser Find. A later gesture-only DOM-to-canvas prototype retained native semantics underneath, but A/B evidence found overlay/cover transition stalls after the actual zoom bottleneck, ancestor-inherited style invalidation, was isolated. It is not an adopted rendering path; its source lineage and evidence harness have been removed.
- Not chosen: full-fidelity passive renders retained for every Card forever; it makes rare far jumps instant by restoring Board-wide preparation and unbounded cache pressure.
- Not chosen: first click only activates and requires a second click for the intended control. The accepted click instead carries its resolved point intent across the one-paint visual handoff; hover is not used to preconstruct Live content.

## Consequences

- ADR 0019 remains authoritative for Detail Band meanings, thresholds, deadband, settle gate, occupancy behavior, and crossfades. Its historical allowance for Cards to load after a fast pan now means detailed Cards may settle over an already-present safety-net representation, never blank space.
- ADR 0020 remains authoritative for board-space shapes, screen-space labels, hit-equals-paint, the pointer-cold scene, and bounded 0–2 gesture proxies. Its inherited Reading rule that visual residency and Live authority are the same set is replaced by `P` versus `L`.
- Pointer hover remains outside the cold Board Scene and outside Live ownership. `G` is the bounded hot geometry/presentation overlay; `L` is a separate owner set and must never be described as bounded by the proxy limit. Mounted DOM hover is CSS-only, and pointer storms cannot trigger full-Board or rich-content work.
- ADR 0010's massive-Board priority, ADR 0011's one-graph/three-projections model, and ADR 0021's removal of visual decay remain accepted and are not reopened.
- No losing renderer remains open as an equivalent production alternative. Buffered `P`, Board-wide `S`, additive Live/first-intent ownership, long-Card continuity, and ADR 0024's bounded image lifecycle ship directly without a feature flag; graph-derived Board-wide semantic entry does not reopen the residency choice or change storage semantics.

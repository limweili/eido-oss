# Chicken evolution: from-scratch primary-source dossier

**Status:** standalone research rerun, current through 5 August 2026

**Provenance:** this synthesis was re-derived from the cited primary literature
without treating the earlier dossier or board as evidence. It is the authority
for the development-board rerun; [`01-primary-source-dossier.md`](./01-primary-source-dossier.md)
is retained only as the historical first iteration.

**Scope:** the decision-useful tree from theropod dinosaurs to domestic chicken,
the living *Gallus* radiation, domestication and introgression, and the
archaeological chronology. This is a self-contained replacement research
dossier for the new board.

**Source rule:** claims below come from original fossil descriptions, phylogenomic analyses, population-genomic studies, direct dating, ancient-DNA work, or published reanalyses of those data. A fossil occurrence is a minimum age; a molecular-clock date is a model estimate; an archaeological phase date is neither a direct bone date nor the start of domestication.

## Synthesis verdict

The independent rerun recovers a stable central account. A chicken is a living
theropod dinosaur; crown birds contain `Palaeognathae + (Galloanserae +
Neoaves)`; the galliform family spine and grey + Sri Lanka junglefowl pairing
are strong; the root of living *Gallus* remains unresolved; domestic chickens
have a principal surviving *G. g. spadiceus*-related source plus extensive
later gene flow; and the genomic population split near 9.5 ka is not a
domestication date.

Six board-critical conclusions make the synthesis current and defensible:

1. **Add *Vegavis*, but as a contested constraint.** A new skull analysis supports a waterfowl placement for the 69.2–68.4 Ma Antarctic bird, older than *Asteriornis*. A 2026 mandibular analysis found no unambiguous anseriform characters and recovered the placement only weakly. The safe card is “Cretaceous waterfowl affinity supported but contested,” not “oldest duck” ([Torres et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08390-0); [Crane et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12862-025-02487-4)).
2. **Update the Jurassic transition.** *Baminornis* was described as the oldest short-tailed, pygostyle-bearing avialan, but the fused series may instead be part of the sacrum. *Zhengheornis* preserves 15 shortened caudals without a pygostyle, directly supporting tail shortening before terminal fusion. Present this as stepwise, mosaic evolution, not a newly found chicken ancestor ([Chen et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08410-z); [Huang et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cretres.2025.106244); [Wang et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeb5202)).
3. **Keep exact crown-bird dates visibly model-dependent.** Large genomic analyses agree on the broad crown-bird backbone but not on deep dates. A family-level genome analysis clusters most neoavian divergences at or just after K–Pg; another clock analysis places much more diversification in the Cretaceous, and a fossil-calibration reanalysis identified problems in 11 of its 20 constraints. Topology is stronger than any single date ([Stiller et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07323-1); [Wu et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2319696121); [Claramunt et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2405887121); [Wu et al. 2024 reply](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2412448121)).
4. **Refine the Ban Non Wat label.** The c. 1650–1250 BCE Neolithic chicken assemblage is phase-associated, not directly radiocarbon-dated bone evidence. The synthesis abstract calls it the first unambiguous domestic-chicken assemblage, but its detailed discussion leaves domestic status unresolved: abundance, juveniles, village association, and rice agriculture support management, while wild and early domestic *Gallus* cannot be cleanly separated osteologically. The board should call this the earliest accepted chicken assemblage in the conservative synthesis, not a directly dated first domestic chicken ([Peters et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2121978119)).
5. **Do not promote the older Yunnan claim.** Caiyuanzi is sometimes reported as chicken at c. 2475–2205 BCE, but the specimen was not figured or directly dated and its identification cannot be evaluated. The claim does not displace Ban Non Wat on the main timeline ([Peng et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2210996119); [Peters et al. 2022 reply](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2213678119)).
6. **Downgrade the pre-Columbian Chile chicken claim.** Re-excavation confirmed that El Arenal 1 contains genuinely pre-/early-contact deposits, but 66 legacy “chicken” bones were morphologically tinamou and all three new DNA-tested candidates were also tinamou. The original directly dated specimen is unavailable for replication. The safe conclusion is “no secure archaeological chicken at the site; the original claim remains unsubstantiated,” not a balanced contact claim ([Buhring et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/15564894.2023.2268551)).

## Board-ready evolutionary backbone

This is a nested tree, not a procession of named ancestors. Daggers mark extinct examples placed beside the route because they reveal anatomy or constrain time.

```text
Archosauria
├── crocodile-line archosaurs
└── bird-line archosaurs
    ├── †Teleocrater and other early side relatives
    └── Dinosauria
        └── Saurischia
            └── Theropoda
                └── Tetanurae
                    └── Coelurosauria
                        └── Maniraptora
                            └── Paraves
                                └── Avialae
                                    ├── †Archaeopteryx-grade branches
                                    ├── †Zhengheornis: short, unfused tail
                                    └── crownward avialans
                                        └── Pygostylia
                                            └── Ornithothoraces
                                                ├── †Enantiornithes
                                                └── Euornithes / ornithuromorph line
                                                    ├── †crownward stem birds
                                                    └── crown birds (Neornithes; naming conventions vary)
                                                        ├── Palaeognathae
                                                        └── Neognathae
                                                            ├── Neoaves
                                                            └── Galloanserae
                                                                ├── Anseriformes
                                                                │   └── †Vegavis? (contested Cretaceous waterfowl)
                                                                └── Galliformes
                                                                    ├── Megapodiidae
                                                                    └── remaining landfowl
                                                                        ├── Cracidae
                                                                        └── remaining landfowl
                                                                            ├── Numididae
                                                                            └── sister pair
                                                                                ├── Odontophoridae
                                                                                └── Phasianidae
                                                                                    └── chickenward path: Gallini
                                                                                        ├── Bambusicola
                                                                                        └── Gallus
                                                                                            ├── G. varius (green; position unresolved)
                                                                                            ├── G. gallus (red; position unresolved)
                                                                                            │   └── principal spadiceus-related domestic source
                                                                                            └── stable sister pair
                                                                                                ├── G. sonneratii (grey)
                                                                                                └── G. lafayettii (Sri Lanka/Ceylon)
```

The crownward stem names are useful waypoints, but their definitions and the placements of fragmentary fossils vary among matrices. In particular, the disputed “pygostyle” of *Baminornis* should not be used to redraw the tree until the anatomy is resolved.

### The living *Gallus* fork must stay unresolved

```text
hypothesis A: (green, (red, (grey, Sri Lanka)))
hypothesis B: (red, (green, (grey, Sri Lanka)))
```

Whole-genome/UCE analyses support hypothesis A, while a multi-individual, contamination-screened SNP analysis supports hypothesis B. Grey + Sri Lanka is stable across them. Rapid radiation, incomplete lineage sorting, ancient gene flow, and captive samples with domestic ancestry explain why a highly supported gene tree need not equal the species history ([Lawal et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-020-0738-1); [Tiley et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40657-020-00194-w); [Mariadassou et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2020.107044)).

## Dates: observed, contextual, and inferred

For the crown-bird root itself, Stiller's official dated tree gives a 93.0 Ma median (85.8–100.9 Ma 95% interval), while a morphology-plus-fossilized-birth–death analysis favours roughly 100 Ma with a 90.3–110.5 Ma interval. A 2025 synthesis found 19 published root estimates spanning 78–133 Ma; that span is a non-independent literature envelope, not a confidence interval ([Stiller et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07323-1); [Brocklehurst & Field 2024](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2023.2618); [McTavish et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2409658122)). The board should show an inferred window, not an exact “first crown bird.”

Two current clock analyses illustrate where the dates are stable and where they are not. Values are median Ma with published 95% intervals; neither column is an observation.

The internal family dates converge far better than the deepest two nodes ([Stiller et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07323-1); [dated-tree dataset](https://doi.org/10.17894/ucph.85624f66-c8e5-4b89-8e8a-fe984ca89e4a); [Chen et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12862-021-01935-1)). Stiller's sampled 28.76 Ma phasianid node is not the full crown Phasianidae because the deepest living phasianid lineage was unsampled. Chen's topology remains strong, but a later osteological analysis questioned its use of *Schaubortyx* as a crown constraint; this affects confidence in dates, not the thousands-of-loci family order ([Zelenkov 2024](https://doi.org/10.1134/S0031030124600860)).

## The domestication overlay belongs beside, not inside, the tree

```text
spadiceus-related red junglefowl  ═════▶  principal surviving domestic lineage

murghi-related red junglefowl    ─ ─ ─▶  South Asian regional ancestry
bankiva/gallus-related red JF    ─ ─ ─▶  Indonesian regional ancestry
jabouillei-related red JF        ─ ─ ─▶  some Chinese regional ancestry

grey junglefowl                  · · ·▶  BCO2 and other candidate tracts
Sri Lanka junglefowl             · · ·▶  a few candidate tracts
green junglefowl                 · · ·▶  one especially compelling candidate tract

domestic chicken                 · · ·▶  modern nominally wild red junglefowl
```

The arrows are not interchangeable. Genome-wide allele sharing can reveal contact but may not establish direction, timing, or a literal ancestry fraction. Long local tracts, topology, divergence, haplotypes, historical genomes, and functional validation strengthen particular edges. Only *BCO2* currently deserves a simple genotype-to-phenotype board card.

## Claims to retire or quarantine

- “*Archaeopteryx*, *Baminornis*, *Asteriornis*, or *Vegavis* evolved into chickens.” They are sampled side relatives or constraints; direct ancestry is not demonstrated.
- “*Baminornis* proves a Jurassic pygostyle.” The original interpretation is plausible but anatomically disputed.
- “*Vegavis* definitively proves ducks existed 69 million years ago.” A waterfowl placement has new support but remains weakly/controversially resolved.
- “Crown birds, Galliformes, or Phasianidae began at one exact molecular-clock date.” Current analyses disagree because calibrations, priors, branch-length methods, and incomplete lineage sorting matter.
- “The *Gallus* species tree is settled.” Print both red-first and green-first hypotheses.
- “Chicken domestication occurred 9,500 years ago at a known site.” The number is a genomic population split; the sampled affinity identifies a broad surviving source range.
- “Non-red junglefowl ancestry proves several independent domestications.” It demonstrates later reticulation unless independent management is separately evidenced.
- “D/f4 values are literal, additive genome percentages.” Closely related donors, incomplete lineage sorting, bidirectional flow, and reference choice violate that shortcut.
- “Ban Non Wat is a directly dated first domestic chicken.” It is the earliest accepted phase-associated assemblage in a conservative synthesis; domestic status is supported contextually but not resolved osteologically.
- “Caiyuanzi proves chickens in Yunnan by 2475–2205 BCE.” The unfigured, indirectly dated identification cannot presently be audited.
- “Rice cultivation caused chicken domestication.” The spatial-temporal association supports a commensal hypothesis; it is not experimental causal proof.
- “El Arenal 1 proves a pre-Columbian Polynesian chicken.” Re-excavation found only tinamou among reassessed and newly tested bird bones, and the original dated specimen is unavailable. The chicken claim is presently unsubstantiated; that does not prove that no pre-contact transfer ever occurred anywhere ([Buhring et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1080/15564894.2023.2268551); [Thomson et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1320412111)).

## Highest-value open questions

1. **Jurassic tail anatomy:** is the fused series of *Baminornis* truly a pygostyle? Re-preparation, high-resolution imaging, and independent character coding would decide whether the earliest pygostyle moved back by about 20 million years.
2. **Cretaceous crown placement:** do expanded, combined morphological matrices robustly retain *Vegavis* within Anseriformes and the newly proposed Antarctic *Pujatopouli* within Neoaves? Until then both belong on a contested-fossil shelf ([Irazoqui et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cretres.2025.106259)).
3. **Clock calibration:** which crown-bird dates survive corrected fossil bounds and coalescent-aware branch lengths? Current simulations show that concatenation can bias dates under incomplete lineage sorting ([Tabatabaee et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/syag038)).
4. **The *Gallus* root:** can ancient/historical samples and explicit phylogenetic-network models distinguish red-first from green-first while modelling incomplete lineage sorting and gene flow together?
5. **First management:** where are directly dated chicken bones and nuclear ancient genomes from the modern *spadiceus* range before and during 2000 BCE? This is the shortest path from broad source range to an actual human–bird process.
6. **Wild reference integrity:** how much of present-day subspecies structure predates domestic backcrossing? Historical museum genomes should become standard references rather than validation afterthoughts.
7. **Trait validation:** beyond *BCO2*, which introgressed or selected variants measurably altered behaviour, reproduction, growth, or immunity, and when did those alleles rise?
8. **Archaeological identification:** can ZooMS, geometric morphometrics, direct dating, and ancient DNA authenticate the unfigured southwestern-China claims and other early phasianid bones?

## Recommended board changes

- Keep the current deep-time spine, galliform tree, unresolved *Gallus* fork, domestication network, and archaeological debate shelf.
- Add three cards: `JURASSIC TAIL — stepwise and disputed`, `VEGAVIS — older but contested`, and `DATE TYPES — fossil / clock / context`.
- Change `Asteriornis — earliest galloanseran` to `Asteriornis — best-supported latest-Cretaceous crown bird near total Galloanserae`; place *Vegavis* above it with an amber question mark.
- Use *Conflicto* at 65.65 Ma as the safest hard minimum for the landfowl–waterfowl crown split; do not silently make *Asteriornis* or *Vegavis* serve that different calibration role.
- Change `Ban Non Wat — earliest secure domestic chicken` to `Ban Non Wat — earliest accepted phase-associated chicken assemblage; domestic status inferential`.
- Put Caiyuanzi and northern China on the amber evidence shelf. Mark El Arenal 1 separately as `original claim unsubstantiated by re-excavation`, not as an equal live alternative on the main timeline.
- Render the species tree and the gene-flow network as separate aligned layers. A thick source arrow plus thinner later introgression arrows communicates the evidence more accurately than a single domestication twig.
